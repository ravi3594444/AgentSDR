Analytics shows how conversations turn into meetings and customers, and which channels bring them in. Open **Analytics** from the top of the sidebar. It is also where you land after you create or choose an organization.

## Pick a period

The date control at the top right sets the period for everything below it. Choose **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** (the default), **Last 90 days**, **This month** or **Last month**, or enter a **Custom range** with a start and end date and choose **Apply range**. A custom range can span at most 180 days.

- Days are whole days in your organization's time zone (**Settings**, **Organization**, **General**), the same zone the assistant's **Overview** uses. **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 days** and **Last 30 days** cover exactly the same days on both pages, so the same figure for the same period is the same number on both.
- A link can name another zone with `?tz=`, for example `?tz=Europe/London`. The page then says it is an override and names your organization's zone beside it.
- Charts show one point a day for ranges up to 62 days, and one point a week for longer ranges.
- The refresh button reloads the numbers, and a label next to the tabs shows how long ago they loaded.
- The page remembers the view and period in its address, so you can bookmark or share a link to it.

Cards that describe the present, such as Needs attention, mailbox capacity and account lists, say "now" or "right now, regardless of the date range" and do not change with the period. **How these figures are counted**, at the foot of the Overview tab, lists each shared definition.

## Compare with the previous period

Most figures carry a change badge. It compares the period you picked with the period of the same length that ends the day before it starts. If you pick 30 days, the comparison is the 30 days before those.

A grey **New** badge means there was nothing in the previous period. A dash means there is no previous data to compare. Green means better and red means worse, and for figures where lower is better, such as median first response, bounces or the AI override rate, a decrease is shown as an improvement.

## Overview

The **Overview** tab starts with the conversations in your CRM and then shows where they came from.

| Figure | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Contacted** | Distinct people with at least one outreach message the provider accepted in the period. Queued, failed and uncertain sends are not sends. |
| **Replies** | Inbound messages received in CRM conversations in the period, on any channel. |
| **Reply rate** | People contacted in the period who replied after their first send in it, divided by people contacted. |
| **Positive** | Distinct CRM records moved into Interested or Customer in the period. |
| **Meetings** | Distinct CRM records moved into a meeting or demo stage in the period. No-shows are not counted. |
| **Customers** | Distinct CRM records moved into Customer in the period. |
| **Median first response** | The median time from a lead's message to your next reply in that conversation. |

Positive, Meetings and Customers count records that moved into the stage during the period, so a record that was already there before the period is not counted again. Below the figures:

- **Replies by sentiment** stacks inbound replies per day or week by the category they were classified into: Interested, Customer, Not interested, Other and Unclassified.
- **Needs attention** is described below.
- **Pipeline now** shows open records by category, and how far along the funnel they are.
- **Stage moves this period** shows records that moved into a new stage, with the top stages.
- **Workflow** shows every record by its current workflow state, such as Action required, Waiting, Paused, Error or Closed.
- **AI assistant** shows drafts generated in the period, how many were sent, drafts sent, replies classified and the override rate: the share of labels a person changed. It also shows how many sent drafts went out unedited, edited or were discarded.

### Channels and funnel

Under **Outreach**, the **Channels** table compares **Email**, **LinkedIn** and **WhatsApp** (and **Instagram** when it has activity) by messages **Sent**, people **Contacted**, **Replied**, **Reply rate** and **Positive**. Select a row to open that channel's tab. Sent counts outreach messages the provider accepted: emails, WhatsApp campaign messages, LinkedIn invitations, acceptance messages and follow-ups, and initial Instagram DMs. Contacted counts distinct people sent at least one of them; Replied counts those people who replied on that channel after their first send in the period. Calls and messages typed on the phone are not outreach sends; the WhatsApp tab counts them separately.

The **Conversion funnel** runs from Contacted to Replied, Positive, Meeting and Customer. Its counts are for the period. They are not one group of people followed from first touch to customer, so a later stage can include people you contacted before the period began.

**Where leads came from** lists, per source and provider (for example **Google Maps through the built-in scraper**, **Instagram through Apify** or **Website search through TinyFish**), the leads **Found**, **Qualified** (saved as a lead: an open business or profile that matched) and **Contactable** (with an open contact route and not Do Not Contact). A person found by several sources is listed under each, and counted once in the total.

## Needs attention

**Needs attention now** lists what is open right now, with a count for each and a link to where you can fix it. These are the same counts the navigation badges and the assistant's Overview show:

- **Needs you**, open decisions waiting for you, such as missing information or a meeting. Interested and opt-out reports are not counted.
- **Action required**, conversations in the queue, opens the queue.
- **Plans waiting for Start**, outreach plans prepared and waiting for you. Nothing is sent for them until you start them.
- **Follow-ups due**, follow-ups drafted or past their time.
- **Drafts to review**, AI replies waiting for approval.
- **Failing mailboxes**, email accounts that cannot send, opens **Settings**, **Mailboxes**.
- **LinkedIn disconnected**, accounts that need reconnecting.
- **WhatsApp disconnected**, numbers that need relinking.

When nothing is open, the card reads **All clear**.

## Channel tabs

Each channel has its own tab with a figure strip, a chart and per-campaign detail.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Email">
    **Emails sent** (first touches and follow-ups the provider accepted), **People contacted** (distinct people sent at least one email), **Replied** (of them, people who replied by email after their first email in the period), **Reply rate** (replied divided by people contacted), **Bounced** and **Unsubscribed** (addresses added to the suppression list in the period).

    Opens and clicks are not recorded, so there are no open or click rates. **Mailbox capacity today** shows what each mailbox has sent against its daily limit, and **Campaigns** lists sends for the period alongside all-time leads, replies and bounces.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="LinkedIn">
    **Invites sent** and **Messages sent** (the acceptance message and follow-ups) count what LinkedIn accepted from the sender. **Accepted** is counted when the accept is recorded, which can be for an earlier invite; **Acceptance rate** is accepted divided by invites sent. **Replied** counts people sent an invitation or message in the period who replied on LinkedIn after it, and **Reply rate** divides them by the people contacted on LinkedIn.

    **Accounts** and **Daily invite capacity** show connection state and today's invitations against each account's limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="WhatsApp">
    **Campaign outreach** covers campaign messages WhatsApp accepted (**Sent**), how many WhatsApp reported **Delivered** and **Read**, **People contacted**, **Replied** and **Reply rate**, defined as on the other tabs. Delivered is not proof that a person saw the message.

    **Calls** covers **Calls placed**, **Connected**, **Connect rate**, **Talk time** and **Avg call**, with **Call outcomes**: Connected, Did not pick up, Not on WhatsApp, Failed and In progress. Calls counted are those placed from AgentSDR.

    **Chat messages** covers **Chat messages out** (every outbound message: campaign sends, replies, and messages typed on the phone or WhatsApp Web), **Chat messages in**, **Chat reply rate** (of chats you wrote in during the period, the share that got a later reply) and **New chats** (started from AgentSDR).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
