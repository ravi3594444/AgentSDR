An email campaign sends a sequence of emails to a list of leads from your connected mailboxes. It sends slowly, at any time of day, and it stops for a lead as soon as that person replies, bounces or unsubscribes. You find campaigns under **Email → Campaigns**, on a page titled **Send campaigns**.

<Info>
Before you start, [connect Google Workspace and add at least one mailbox](/email/connect). A campaign cannot launch without a connected mailbox.
</Info>

## Create a campaign

Click **Create campaign**. The wizard has four steps, shown on the left: **Campaign details**, **Add leads**, **Build sequence** and **Review & launch**.

<Frame caption="Step 1 of 4. The steps are listed on the left with a running summary below them.">
  <img src="/assets/screenshots/app/email-campaign-new.png" alt="The Create a send campaign wizard on step 1 of 4, Campaign details, asking for a campaign name" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Campaign name**, an internal name such as "Q3 Enterprise Outbound". A draft is saved when you continue. Going back edits the same draft instead of creating another.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add leads">
    Choose **Add from People** to reuse contacts already in your lead database (people already in the campaign are skipped), or **Upload CSV or XLSX**. For a file, you review a column mapping before anything is imported. See [Import leads](/leads/import) for the file format. Email is required.

    After an import you see a summary: rows, imported, duplicate, suppressed and failed. Suppressed means the address is on the suppression list, so it is never imported into the campaign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build the sequence">
    Write each email, set the waits between them, and preview it. Details below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and launch">
    The review shows the lead count, the number of steps and how many mailboxes are connected. Click **Launch campaign**, or **Save as draft** to come back later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

**Launch campaign** is disabled until there is a connected mailbox, a complete sequence and at least one lead. You can also launch a draft from the campaign page.

## Build the sequence

The sequence editor lists each email as **Email step 1**, **Email step 2** and so on. Select a step to edit its **Subject** and **Email body**.

- Step 1 is the **Initial email**. It needs a subject.
- Click **Add follow-up** for more steps. Each follow-up has a **Wait** field: the number of days after the previous step it sends. New follow-ups default to 3 days, and the minimum is 1.
- Every step needs a body. **Save** is blocked with a message if one is empty.
- Click **Preview** on a step to see it rendered for a real lead and a real mailbox.

### Threading

Leave a follow-up's **Subject** blank ("Leave blank to continue the same thread") and it is sent as a reply in the same thread, with `Re:` added to the earlier subject. If you write a subject, that step is sent as a new, separate email.

### Merge fields

Click **Insert field** above the body to pick from a searchable list of fields. The list comes from the campaign's own leads, so every column of an uploaded file appears. Fields look like `{{firstName}}`.

Always available are `{{firstName}}`, `{{lastName}}` and `{{company}}`. Other things you can use include `{{email}}`, `{{title}}` and `{{companyDomain}}`, plus any extra column from your import. A column the importer does not recognise is turned into a camelCase key, so "Job Title" becomes `{{jobTitle}}`. Matching ignores upper and lower case.

<Warning>
A merge field with no value does not fail and is not left as text. It is replaced with an empty string, exactly like a lead with no first name. A missing value can leave "Hi ," in the email, so use **Preview** on several leads before you launch.
</Warning>

### Variants and signature

- `{A|B|C}` picks one option at random for each email, so emails to different leads are not identical.
- Click **Signature** to insert `%signature%`, which is replaced with the sending mailbox's [signature](/email/connect). With no placeholder, the signature is appended.

Every email also gets an unsubscribe link at the bottom, which you do not need to add yourself.

## How sending works

Sending is deliberately slow and spread out.

- **Mailboxes are one shared pool.** A campaign does not pick mailboxes. Every connected mailbox in your organization can send for any active campaign. New leads are shared across mailboxes in turn, and a lead then stays with the mailbox that sent its first email, so follow-ups come from the same address.
- **A queue is built once a day.** Each mailbox's queue for the day is filled first with follow-ups that are due (most overdue first), then with new leads, up to its daily limit. The daily counters also reset at that point.
- **Then emails go out one at a time.** About once a minute, each mailbox with queued leads sends one email, if it is outside its random gap (18 to 24 minutes by default). Mailboxes send at any time of day.
- **Limits are automatic, and one is yours.** Each mailbox's daily limit and gap are applied automatically; a first email to someone no channel has contacted before also takes a place under your workspace's [daily lead limit](/workspace/sending-rules).

<Note>
Because the queue is built once a day, a campaign launched mid-day may not send until the next build. The build runs from a scheduled job inside the app, at the start of your organization's day (Settings, Organization, Defaults: Time zone and New day starts at).
</Note>

A step is sent at most once per lead. If sending fails, the email is marked **Failed** instead of being retried automatically, and the campaign's analytics show it under **Failed sends** with a hint to check the mailbox.

## Start, pause and resume

The campaign page header shows its status: **Draft**, **Active**, **Paused** or **Completed**.

- **Pause** stops further sends right away, including emails already queued for the day.
- **Resume** (on a paused campaign) starts it again.
- Campaigns can be deleted from the list.

Pausing does not change leads' positions. They continue from the step they were on.

## What stops a lead's sequence

A lead leaves the sequence, and nothing more is sent to them, when:

- **They reply.** When a reply from a lead arrives, the lead becomes **Replied**, their queued emails are removed, and the reply goes to [Action required](/crm/action-required). This applies to a reply on any channel, so an answer on LinkedIn also stops their email follow-ups. Reply detection needs the Gmail Pub/Sub topic described in [Connect email](/email/connect).
- **Their email bounces.** AgentSDR reads delivery failure reports in your mailbox, marks the lead **Bounced**, and adds the address to the suppression list. A temporary "delay" notice does not suppress anyone, because delivery may still succeed.
- **They unsubscribe.** Every email has an **Unsubscribe** link and a one-click unsubscribe header, so Gmail and Outlook can show their own button. Either way the address is added to the suppression list and the lead becomes **Suppressed**.
- **They are marked Do Not Contact** anywhere in your workspace. This is checked again right before each send.
- **The sequence ends.** After the last step the lead becomes **Completed**.

A suppressed address is never mailed again from any campaign in your organization.

## Track results

Open a campaign for three tabs.

- **Analytics**: leads, contacted, emails sent, replied, bounced and failed sends, where leads stand, send activity over time and sends by step. Replies are counted for the campaign as a whole, not per step.
- **Leads**: filter by **Pending**, **In sequence**, **Replied**, **Completed**, **Bounced** or **Suppressed**, search by name, email or company, and import more leads.
- **Sequence**: edit the steps. Changing a sequence on a live campaign affects the steps that have not yet sent.

The cards above the tabs show follow-ups due today, new leads reached today and when the next email is due.
