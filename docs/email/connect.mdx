Email in AgentSDR sends and reads Gmail through a Google Workspace service account. You connect that account once for your organization, then add the individual mailboxes (for example `rep@yourcompany.com`) that campaigns send from. All of it lives under **Settings → Email**, which has three tabs: **Mailboxes**, **Connection** and **Sending rules**.

<Info>
Only Google Workspace mailboxes are supported today. Microsoft Outlook is listed on the **Connection** tab as "coming soon".
</Info>

## Connect Google Workspace

Open **Settings → Email → Connection**. The **Google Workspace** card shows **Connected** or **Not connected**, and what it powers: email accounts, campaigns and reply sync.

<Frame caption="The Connection tab. Connect on the Google Workspace card opens the setup dialog.">
  <img src="/assets/screenshots/app/email-connection.png" alt="Settings, Email, Connection tab showing a Google Workspace card marked Not connected with a Connect button, and a Microsoft Outlook card marked Coming soon" />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect">
    The card opens a form with a **How to connect** guide. It lists the Google Cloud and Google Admin steps: enable the Gmail API, create a service account and a JSON key, then authorise the service account for the Gmail scopes in the Admin console (domain-wide delegation). The full walkthrough is in [Google Workspace setup](/integrations/google-workspace).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the credentials">
    Use **Upload key file** to fill the fields from the JSON key, or paste them by hand.

    - **Service account email** (required)
    - **Private key** (required)
    - **Gmail Pub/Sub topic** (optional)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    AgentSDR checks that Google accepts the key before it saves, so a typo fails on the form rather than on your first send. A topic that is filled in must look like `projects/<project>/topics/<topic>`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Without the **Gmail Pub/Sub topic**, sending works but replies are not picked up. Leave it empty only if you do not need reply detection, because reply detection is what stops a sequence when someone answers.
</Warning>

Once connected, the card shows **Edit**, **Disconnect** and when the credentials were last verified. Disconnect pauses everything that depends on Google: email pages show a **Connect Google Workspace** prompt, background sending stops, and nothing is read from env variables as a fallback. Owners and admins manage this; see [Organizations](/workspace/organizations).

## Add a mailbox

The credentials above let AgentSDR act for your domain. A mailbox is one address on that domain.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Mailboxes">
    Go to **Settings → Email → Mailboxes** and click **Add mailbox**. Until Google is connected this page shows the Connect prompt instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the address">
    The dialog is titled **Add a mailbox**.

    - **Email address** (required): a Google Workspace address covered by your domain-wide delegation.
    - **Display name** (optional): the name recipients see beside the address.
    - **Signature** (optional): added to every send from this mailbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect and test">
    Click **Connect & test**. AgentSDR calls Gmail as that address. If it works you see **Mailbox connected**. If not, **The connection test failed** shows Google's reason and you can click **Try again**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

An address can belong to only one organization. If it is already connected elsewhere, the dialog says so.

## Read the Mailboxes table

Each row shows a status and how many emails went out today against the mailbox's daily limit. Mailboxes send at any time of day, every day; there are no sending hours to set.

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Connected** | Can send. |
| **Failed** | The last connection test failed. The error appears under the address; click **Test again**. |
| **Connecting** | The first test has not finished. |
| **Disabled** | Not sending. |

Only **Connected** mailboxes send. The cards above the table summarise **Connected**, **Needs attention**, **Sent today** and **Left today** (daily limits of connected mailboxes minus what they sent). Failed mailboxes are sorted to the top.

Use the **...** menu on a row for **Test connection**, **Send test email** (connected mailboxes only), **Signature** and **Remove mailbox**. Removing a mailbox stops it sending, and emails scheduled from it do not go out.

## Daily limit

A new mailbox starts with the organization's emails-per-mailbox pacing, 30 a day by default. The table shows each mailbox's sent-today count against its own limit, and the queue never schedules more than that limit in a day. This pacing is automatic: there is no setting for it. Above about 50 a day from one Google Workspace mailbox, deliverability usually drops and Google may throttle it.

New people a mailbox emails also count toward your [daily lead limit](/workspace/sending-rules), shared by every channel.

## Signature

Choose **Signature** on a mailbox to edit it. It is plain text, such as a name and title. In a sequence, put `%signature%` where it should appear; if the email body has no placeholder, the signature is appended at the end. If the mailbox has no signature, the placeholder is removed rather than sent literally.

## Pacing

Mailboxes send at any time, every day. Each mailbox waits at least 30 seconds between sends by default. Queue checks, delivery processing, daily limits and provider restrictions can add more time. See [Sending rules](/workspace/sending-rules).
