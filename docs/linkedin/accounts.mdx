LinkedIn campaigns send connection requests and messages from LinkedIn accounts that you connect through Unipile. You connect Unipile once for your organization, then add each LinkedIn profile from **Settings → LinkedIn**. That section has two tabs: **Accounts** and **Connection**.

<Info>
Automating LinkedIn is against LinkedIn's terms and can get an account restricted. Read [Responsible use](/responsible-use) first. The limits on this page exist to keep activity modest, but no tool can promise an account will never be limited.
</Info>

## Connect Unipile

Open **Settings → LinkedIn → Connection**. The **Unipile** card shows **Connected** or **Not connected**.

<Frame caption="The Connection tab before Unipile is connected.">
  <img src="/assets/screenshots/app/linkedin-connection.png" alt="Settings, LinkedIn, Connection tab showing the Unipile card marked Not connected with a Connect button, above a note that the same connection powers WhatsApp" />
</Frame>

1. Click **Connect** (or **Edit**) and open **How to connect** for the steps.
2. Enter the **DSN (API address)** and **Access token** from your Unipile dashboard. Save.

AgentSDR checks the credentials with Unipile before saving. It then registers the webhooks it needs in your Unipile workspace itself, so accepted invitations and replies arrive here. The card's **Webhooks** line shows the result, with **Register again** if it failed and **Show URLs and secret** if you would rather register them by hand. The full credential steps are in [Unipile setup](/integrations/unipile).

The same Unipile connection also powers WhatsApp. Changing it here changes it there too.

Until Unipile is connected, **Accounts** shows a **Connect Unipile** prompt instead of the list.

## Add a LinkedIn account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Connect LinkedIn account">
    On **Settings → LinkedIn → Accounts**, the button is at the top right. It opens Unipile's hosted sign-in in the same tab and shows **Opening LinkedIn…** while it redirects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in to LinkedIn on Unipile's page">
    Complete LinkedIn's sign-in and any verification (captcha, code). Unipile hosts this step, so AgentSDR never sees your LinkedIn password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to AgentSDR">
    You are sent back to the Accounts page, which shows **Syncing the connected account from Unipile…** and then lists the account. If the wizard did not complete you see **LinkedIn was not connected**. Try again.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If sync fails after a successful sign-in, you see **Connected, but the sync failed**. The account appears after the next outreach run.

## Read the Accounts page

The cards at the top show **Connected**, **Needs attention**, **Requests today** and **Premium**. Each account row shows:

| Column | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Account** | Name and username, with a Premium badge for Premium or Sales Navigator accounts. |
| **Status** | **Connected**, **Limit reached** or **Disconnected**. |
| **Requests today** | Connection requests sent today against the account's daily limit. "Own limit" appears when the account has a limit of its own. |
| **Pending** | Leads waiting for a connection request from this account. |
| **Next run** | When the account next works, after its rest between runs. |

What the statuses mean:

- **Connected**: the account can send.
- **Limit reached**: LinkedIn refused something because of a limit. The account stops for the day and resumes by itself. See [LinkedIn campaigns](/linkedin/campaigns).
- **Disconnected**: LinkedIn signed the session out or Unipile lost it. Campaigns skip the account until you reconnect. Click **Reconnect** under the status, or choose **Reconnect account** in the row menu. This re-authenticates the same account in place, so its campaigns and history stay.

## Daily connection request limit

Each account sends at most a set number of connection requests a day. The limit is automatic and depends on the account type:

| Account | Per day |
| --- | --- |
| Premium or Sales Navigator | 30 |
| Free | 5 |

An account that was given its own limit before these became automatic keeps it; the table marks it "Own limit". New people an account invites also count toward your [daily lead limit](/workspace/sending-rules), shared by every channel.

<Warning>
LinkedIn restricts accounts that send much more than 30 invitations a day, and free accounts have a much smaller weekly allowance.
</Warning>

## Any time of day

Accounts work at any time, every day: there are no working hours to set. Beyond the daily limit, accounts send in short runs and then rest. Each run sends a few invitations with a gap between them, then the account waits before its next run; **Next run** shows when. The defaults and how the engine uses them are covered in [LinkedIn campaigns](/linkedin/campaigns).
