This page assumes AgentSDR is already running and you can open it in a browser. If not, start with [Self-hosting](/self-hosting). You will create your organization, connect a channel and the AI provider, import leads and launch a first email campaign.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account and organization">
    On a fresh instance, the first visit opens the **Set up AgentSDR** page. Enter **Your name**, **Organization name**, **Email** and **Password**, then choose **Create admin account**. You become the organization's owner, are signed in and land on Analytics. No confirmation email is needed for this first account.

    <Info>
    By default an instance is invite-only: everyone after the first account needs an invitation from an existing organization. Invited people confirm their email address, so set up [Resend](/integrations/resend) before inviting anyone. See [Organizations and members](/workspace/organizations).
    </Info>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your AI provider">
    Go to **Settings**, then **AI provider**. Connect OpenRouter with an inference key and a management key, pick the providers and models you want, choose a default model and confirm that shared capacity is never used. Until you save this, AI features such as reply classification and drafts cannot run. The full walkthrough is in [AI provider](/workspace/ai-provider).

    <Frame caption="Settings, AI provider is where you connect OpenRouter.">
      <img src="/assets/screenshots/app/ai-provider.png" alt="Settings, AI provider page with numbered sections for the OpenRouter account, providers and models, and the default model, and a Connect OpenRouter button" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel">
    Each channel has its own set of tabs under **Settings**, and its service is connected on the tab for it. A feature switches on only once its service is connected.

    - **Email.** On **Email**, open the **Connection** tab and connect your Google Workspace service account. Then open **Mailboxes** and choose **Add mailbox**. See [Connect email](/email/connect).
    - **LinkedIn.** On **LinkedIn**, open **Connection** and connect Unipile, then link an account on the **Accounts** tab. See [LinkedIn accounts](/linkedin/accounts).
    - **WhatsApp.** On **WhatsApp**, open **Integrations** and connect Unipile (and Cloudflare R2 if you will record calls), then choose **Sync from Unipile** on the **Numbers** tab. See [WhatsApp accounts](/whatsapp/accounts).

    Credentials for each service are in [Integrations](/integrations).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import your leads">
    Open **Leads** and choose **Import people**. Pick an `.xlsx`, `.xls` or `.csv` file; you can start from **Download Excel template**. Columns beyond the basics become merge fields you can use in messages. See [Import leads](/leads/import).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch a first campaign">
    Under **Outreach**, open **Email**, then **Campaigns**, and choose **Create campaign**. The wizard has four steps: **Campaign details**, **Add leads**, **Build sequence** and **Review & launch**.

    <Frame caption="The four-step campaign wizard.">
      <img src="/assets/screenshots/app/email-campaign-new.png" alt="The Create a send campaign wizard on step 1 of 4, Campaign details, asking for a campaign name" />
    </Frame>

    **Launch campaign** stays disabled until the campaign has a connected mailbox, a complete sequence and at least one lead. You can also choose **Save as draft** and come back. See [Email campaigns](/email/campaigns).

    <Tip>
    Start with a small list. New mailboxes send up to 30 emails a day, spaced by a random gap, at any time of day, and your workspace contacts at most 50 new people a day by default (the daily lead limit). See [Sending rules](/workspace/sending-rules).
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work the replies">
    When someone replies, the message lands in **Action required** in the sidebar, together with the AI's classification and a draft answer. Review the draft, edit it if you want, and send it. Replies are also visible in the channel's own inbox: **Email**, **Inbox**; **LinkedIn**, **Messages**; **WhatsApp**, **Messages**.

    **Analytics** then shows replies, positive replies, meetings and customers for the period. See [Action required](/crm/action-required) and [Analytics](/analytics).
  </Step>
</Steps>
