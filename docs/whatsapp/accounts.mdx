AgentSDR messages over WhatsApp from numbers you link in Unipile. Each number has a status, a warm-up period after linking and its own daily limit for starting new chats. This page covers linking, syncing and the guards that every WhatsApp send passes through.

## Before you start

WhatsApp messaging and calling run on your organization's Unipile connection, the same one LinkedIn uses. Connect it first on **Settings → WhatsApp → Integrations**. Until it is connected, the Numbers page shows a Connect prompt instead of your numbers. Setup details are in [Unipile setup](/integrations/unipile).

## Link a number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Link it in Unipile">
    In the Unipile dashboard choose **Accounts → Connect → WhatsApp** and scan the QR code with the phone that owns the number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Numbers">
    In AgentSDR go to **Settings → WhatsApp → Numbers**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync">
    Click **Sync from Unipile**. AgentSDR reads the WhatsApp numbers Unipile has and lists them. If Unipile has none yet, the page says so.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Sync reads only WhatsApp accounts, never your LinkedIn ones. A number that Unipile no longer lists is marked **Disconnected** at the next sync.

The first time AgentSDR sees a number connected, it records that moment. The warm-up below counts from it.

## What the Numbers page shows

A strip at the top counts your numbers:

- **Connected**, out of all linked numbers.
- **Needs attention**: numbers that are disconnected, need credentials or report an error. Relink these in Unipile, then sync.
- **Warming up**: numbers still inside their warm-up.
- **New chats a day**: the total your connected numbers may start per day, with the per-number limit and the gap between sends underneath.

Each number has one of four statuses: **Connected**, **Needs credentials**, **Disconnected** or **Error**. Only a connected number can send. A number still warming up shows "Warming up until" and the date.

<Warning>
A number that WhatsApp bans also loses its calling. That is why the guards below are enforced by the server, not only by the interface.
</Warning>

## Number actions

Open the three-dot menu on a row.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Make default** | Sends go from this number when nothing else picks one. The default shows a **Default** badge. |
| **Import recent chats** | Imports up to 100 recent chats and the last 30 messages of each from Unipile. It is history only, so nothing reaches the CRM. Safe to run again. Needs a connected number. |
| **New chats a day** | Sets a limit for this number only. See below. |

By default a message goes out from the number that is already chatting with that person. If there is no such number, it uses the default one.

### Set a limit for one number

Choose **New chats a day** on the number. Enter a value, or leave the field empty to follow the organization's rule, then click **Save limit**. **Use organization limit** clears an override you set before. The allowed range is 1 to 200. The dialog warns you above 25.

## The guards

Every WhatsApp send passes through the same checks: replies from the Messages page, CRM replies, [message campaigns](/whatsapp/campaigns) and call follow-ups. They run on the server under a per-number lock, so two sends at the same moment cannot both slip through.

| Guard | Default | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm-up after linking | 24 hours | A new number answers existing chats right away but starts no new chats until the warm-up ends. A number AgentSDR has never seen connected cannot start new chats at all. Sync, then try again. |
| New chats per 24 hours | 25 per number | Once a number has started this many new chats in a rolling 24 hours, further new chats are refused. Existing chats can still be answered. |
| Gap between sends | 10 seconds | A second message from the same number sooner than this is refused with a wait time. |
| Do Not Contact | Always on | A message to a lead marked Do Not Contact is refused. |

A "new chat" means a number this account has never messaged. Chats the lead started, or that you started from your phone, do not count against the limit. The day is a rolling 24 hours rather than a calendar day, so sending 25 at 23:59 and 25 more at 00:01 is not possible.

Other refusals you may see: the number is not connected, the message is empty, or the message is longer than 4096 characters.

### Automatic pacing

The numbers above are applied automatically: 24 hours of warm-up, 25 new chats per number in any rolling 24 hours, and 10 seconds between messages from one number. There is no setting for them, and no sending hours: numbers send at any time of day. A number that already had its own **New chats a day** keeps it.

A first message to someone no channel has contacted before also takes one place under your workspace's [daily lead limit](/workspace/sending-rules). Once today's places are used, first messages to new people wait for the reset; follow-ups and replies go on.

<Tip>
WhatsApp bans numbers that start many chats with people who do not have them saved, which is why new numbers warm up and every number starts new chats slowly.
</Tip>

## Messages and where they come from

Chats on your linked numbers appear under **WhatsApp → Messages**. Each message has an origin:

- **lead**: the person wrote it.
- **agentsdr**: AgentSDR sent it through Unipile.
- **phone**: an echo of something you typed on your phone or in WhatsApp Web, which AgentSDR did not send.

A reply from a lead you already know by phone lands in the [CRM](/crm/overview) on a WhatsApp conversation. A message you send from your phone also counts as answering the lead.
