AgentSDR sends at any time of day, every day. There are no sending hours or sending days to set, for mailboxes, LinkedIn accounts, WhatsApp numbers or Instagram accounts: an approved message that is due goes out as soon as its sender's pacing allows.

The one sending setting you choose is the **daily lead limit**. Everything else that paces sending is applied automatically, with defaults chosen to keep your accounts safe.

## The daily lead limit

**Settings → Daily lead limit** sets **Maximum new leads to contact per day**: how many people AgentSDR may contact for the first time each day, across email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp and Instagram together.

| Setting | Default | Min | Max | Safe edge |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maximum new leads to contact per day | 50 | 1 | 1,000 | Warns above 200 |

- **A person counts once**, when the first automated message to them starts sending: the first email of a campaign, a LinkedIn connection request, the first WhatsApp campaign message or an initial Instagram DM. Someone already contacted on any channel does not count again when another channel reaches them.
- **What does not count:** follow-ups, replies (yours or the assistant's) and messages you send yourself.
- **The day** is your workspace's own: the **Time zone** and **New day starts at** on **Settings → Your business**. The page shows today's count against the limit, and when it next resets.
- **When the limit is reached**, first messages to new people wait until the reset; nothing is lost or skipped. Follow-ups, replies, and first messages to people already contacted on another channel still go out.
- **Nothing resets early.** The count is read from the messages AgentSDR has actually started, so a restart or a second server does not grant more. A message whose delivery is uncertain keeps its place; one that definitely did not go out (for example, stopped by Do Not Contact) frees its place again.

Edit the value and choose **Save changes**. **Use default** puts it back to 50. Only owners and admins can change it; members see it read-only.

## Pacing that is automatic

Each sender is also paced on its own, so no mailbox, profile or number moves faster than its platform tolerates. These limits have no settings page; they apply automatically, alongside the daily lead limit.

| Channel | Pacing | Default |
|---|---|---|
| Email | Emails per day, per mailbox | 30 |
| Email | Gap between emails from one mailbox | 18 to 24 minutes, random |
| LinkedIn | Connection requests per day, per account | 30 with Premium or Sales Navigator, 5 on a free plan |
| LinkedIn | Requests per run, then a rest | 3 to 4, then 30 to 60 minutes |
| LinkedIn | Delay between requests | 30 to 60 seconds |
| WhatsApp | Warm-up after linking a number | 24 hours with no new chats |
| WhatsApp | New chats per number | 25 in any rolling 24 hours |
| WhatsApp | Gap between messages from one number | 10 seconds |
| Instagram | Initial DMs per account | 10 in any rolling 24 hours |
| Instagram | Gap between initial DMs | 5 to 10 minutes |

A limit an account was given before these became automatic (a mailbox's own daily limit, a LinkedIn account's own request limit, a WhatsApp number's own new chats) keeps applying. A platform's own restrictions (LinkedIn's weekly invitation allowance, WhatsApp rate limits) are respected as they come: the sender waits and tries again later, without failing your leads.

Whatever the limits say, AgentSDR never contacts someone marked Do Not Contact.

## The pages these replaced

The per-channel **Sending rules** pages (Email, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Instagram) and the per-mailbox and per-account **Sending hours** and **Working hours** are gone. Old links to those pages open **Settings → Daily lead limit**. The time zone, the time a new day starts and the default phone country moved to **Settings → Your business**.
