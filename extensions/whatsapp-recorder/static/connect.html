Connect this browser to 22nd x ai

The 22nd x ai Browser Assistant works in this browser, signed in as you: it opens tabs (grouped and in its own task window), reads pages and, when you ask it in the chat, sends, posts, fills in or buys what you asked for. Your passwords, full card numbers and sign-in codes stay in this browser.

Website access

App: …

Website access is only where the assistant may go. What it does there is what you ask in the chat: a request to research only reads; sending, posting, filling in or buying happens only when you asked for it. Contacting people still goes through your outreach approval or Autonomous mode, Do Not Contact and your sending limits.

Some sites' terms restrict automated browsing. The assistant acts as you, so a site may limit or question your account; give access only where you accept that.

Chrome asks you once to allow this access. You can stop, change the websites or disconnect at any time from the extension's toolbar button.
