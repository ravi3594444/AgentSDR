Browser assistant

Once you press Connect browser in the 22nd x ai chat, the assistant opens background tabs in this Chrome, signed in as you, on the websites you allow. It reads them, and changes something there only when you ask for that task in the chat. Contacting people stays in your approved outreach.

Calls and recording

When you press Call in 22nd x ai, the extension opens the chat in WhatsApp Web in this Chrome, presses Voice call and records the call from pickup to hang-up: your microphone and the other side. The recording is uploaded to your workspace, which keeps it on the lead and can transcribe it. Only calls placed from 22nd x ai are recorded; your own WhatsApp calls are never touched.

  • Keep WhatsApp Web signed in, in English: the extension finds its Voice call and End call buttons by their English labels.
  • Calls work from the 22nd x ai addresses below, the same as the browser assistant.
  • After updating the extension, reload WhatsApp Web before your next call.
